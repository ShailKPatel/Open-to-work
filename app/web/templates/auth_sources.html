Authenticated job sources

Fetch a job posting from a site you have to be signed in to see (Wellfound, LinkedIn, and similar). This automates a real browser login with the credentials you give it below.

Read this before adding one

  • Automated login is against most sites' terms of service. Repeated or heavy use can get the signed-in account flagged, rate-limited, or permanently banned.
  • Use a throwaway or alternate account, never your primary one.
  • This is fragile: any change to the site's login form, a CAPTCHA, or two-factor auth will break it with no warning, and it can't work around any of those.
  • Your username and password are encrypted at rest and only ever decrypted in memory for the moment of a real login attempt. No session or cookie is kept between fetches; every fetch logs in fresh and closes the browser afterward.

CSS selectors for the login form's fields. Inspect the page in your browser (right-click → Inspect) to find them.
